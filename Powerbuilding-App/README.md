# Powerbuilding Log

A personal training, nutrition and men's physique prep log that installs on iPhone and desktop.

- **Workout:** 5–6 day split, sets and reps with check-offs, 1RM and training max table,
  % based loading, and the weights you lifted last time.
- **Progress:** weekly totals, strength trend, nutrition adherence, backup and restore.
- **Nutrition:** carb cycling targets, a meal-by-meal macro log, USDA food search, body weight.
- **Prep:** show countdown, rate of loss, daily check, posing practice, weekly photo check-ins,
  cardio and steps.

Data syncs through your own Supabase project. Setup steps are in [SETUP.md](SETUP.md).
